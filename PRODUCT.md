# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors exploring Benjamin P. Haddon's work across narrative, systems, and psyche. They need to understand each throughline, inspect projects, and reach live work on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

An interactive portfolio that turns mythic frameworks, attention-respecting progressive web apps, and psychological explorations into tangible examples. Success means visitors can discover relevant projects and understand the ideas behind them.

## Brand Personality

Mythic, reflective, experimental. The existing portfolio uses a cinematic introduction and three distinct throughlines to invite exploration.

## Anti-references

Avoid interchangeable corporate portfolio templates, attention traps, and effects that prevent access to the work.

## Design Principles

- Let the projects demonstrate the ideas.
- Preserve the distinct character of the three throughlines.
- Respect attention through responsive, efficient interactions.
- Preserve the current visual identity during performance work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep content usable across desktop and mobile. Preserve keyboard navigation, readable content, and image descriptions. Motion should respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Context provenance

Inferred from README.md and existing interface for the optimize task. Audience and personality remain open to refinement by the owner.
